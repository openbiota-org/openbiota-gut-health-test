THE RESEARCH CATALOG

The research behind
the first release.

OpenBiota was built by reading the science and connecting it to your own data. This is the complete list of what we read.

Every published study, dataset, genetic catalog, reference database, and clinical guidance document recorded in the OpenBiota build specifications—versions v0.1.0 through v0.8.3—is listed below. For each one you can see what it is, how it informed the software, the institutions behind it, where the work took place, and a link to the source itself.

Nothing here is hidden behind a login or a paywall of ours. Follow any link and read the research yourself. If a source supports a finding in your report, you deserve to be able to check it.

How to read this catalog

What it is

A plain-language summary of the source: the study design, the population or model, or what a dataset and database actually contains.

How it informed OpenBiota

What the work contributed—an analysis method, a reference catalog, a disease pattern, an evidence card—and the limits we kept attached to it.

Institutions & country

The principal institutions and the location the work is attributed to. Where the record could not establish one, the field is left empty rather than guessed.

Notes & provenance

Open a record to see corrections, retractions, contradictions, interpretation limits, archive accessions, and which build specification first cited it.
