## What the Output actually is

A research observation about DNA sequence in a sample, placed against reference
cohorts. Specifically:

- **Measured capacity is not measured activity.** Gene content says what a
  community *could* do, not what it is doing.
- **Resemblance is not disease.** A high score against a published
  disease-associated pattern is a research observation, not a diagnosis.
- **A percentile is not a reference range.** It says where you sit among
  reference samples, not what is healthy for you.
- **A detected sequence is not an infection.**
- **An evidence record is not a finding about you.** Where the report describes
  research on a food, compound, supplement, botanical, probiotic, drug, product,
  protocol, clinical route or procedure, it is *describing published research* —
  not recommending, endorsing, or guaranteeing that it is safe, suitable,
  effective, legal or available for you.
