# Code of conduct

This project is about people's health. The people who bring their samples,
their questions and their work here deserve to be treated with care.

## Expected

- Be direct and be kind. Criticise the work, never the person.
- Assume good faith; ask before concluding.
- Respect privacy absolutely: never share, request or speculate about any
  individual's data, health or identity, including your own contributors'.
- Give credit: cite the study, name the author, link the source.
- Say what you do not know. A claim in this project carries its evidence; a
  contributor who says "I am not sure" is doing it right.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, discrimination or personal attacks of any kind.
- Medical advice presented as fact, or discouraging anyone from seeking care
  from a clinician.
- Publishing or linking to any person's sequencing data or report without
  their explicit, documented consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussion, or retaliation against anyone who
  reports a problem.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to **conduct@openbiota.com**. Reports are read by the
maintainers, kept confidential, and answered within seven days. Consequences
range from a private conversation to removal from the project, at the
maintainers' judgement and in proportion to the harm.

Adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1, with the privacy obligations this project's subject demands.
